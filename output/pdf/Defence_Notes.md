# Five-minute presentation and live demo

- 0:00–0:30 — Slide 1: title, team and objective.
- 0:30–1:10 — Slide 2: movement, turn-based battle and repeated floors.
- 1:10–1:45 — Slide 3: show the three independently switchable effects.
- 1:45–2:35 — Slide 4: explain shared enemy coordinates, noise, threshold,
  discard and the same mask in the shadow pass.
- 2:35–3:15 — Slide 5: explain rim dot product, PCF and continuous animation.
- 3:15–4:00 — Slide 6: real FPS results, G fallback and test coverage.
- 4:00–5:00 — Slide 7 and live demo: F2, T, R, B, J/L, P; then return
  to gameplay and show one battle/upgrade. Keep a separate 60-second backup.

The seven slides are a guide; practise the complete slot on the actual computer.
Questions take another two minutes. All members need to explain the headline
shader, not only their own module. Practise changing edge width 0.09, noise
frequency 5.0, dissolve duration 0.9, rim power 4.5 and the shadow bias.
